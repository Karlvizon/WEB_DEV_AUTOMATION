# QA feedback from past revisions

QA comments collected from monday (Page Design, Final MLP and Final Mobile Check subitems), grouped so the
same comment does not come back. **Read this before every build and every revision.**

How to use it:
- The Figma is still the source of truth. When the Figma breaks one of these points, **don't change it silently**.
  Build from the Figma and log the point as a Question in the tracker, quoting the rule below.
- Points that are about the build itself (mobile layout, spacing, sliders) are applied directly.
- After every revision, add any new point here: general, brand or dealer, with the date and MLP.

## General (every MLP)

- **Model year.** Every heading, trim title and note must say the page's model year (Canyon, 2026-11-02: "this is already a 2027 model, please update all the 2026 notes"). Flag any other year in the Figma copy.
- **Duplicate disclaimers.** The same disclaimer must not appear twice in the list (Gladiator, Durango, Ram 2500, Compass, Encore GX, Envista, Blazer). Check before export and flag duplicates.
- **Asterisks.** Each claim with a disclaimer gets a `*`; use `*` instead of the word "Disclosure" (Pacifica, Durango). Put the star right after "Base MSRP*" or "Starting MSRP*", not elsewhere (bZ). Package and group names get one too: "Family Tech Group*" (2027 Chrysler Pacifica, Huston CDJR, 2026-11-12: "Please add an asterisk (*) after the word \"Group\"").
- **Trims.** Must match the OEM **Build & Price** page and include every trim in both drive types (GMC/Chevrolet: 2WD and 4WD; Buick: FWD and AWD) (Trax, Canyon, Encore GX, Envista, 2026-11-06). If the Figma trims are short, flag it.
- **Content from the OEM.** Headlines, titles and taglines follow the OEM site; flag repeated or wrong title/tagline pairs (Gladiator, Durango, Santa Fe, Corvette).
- **Discover section.** The background must show the dealer's own brand (Encore GX / Envista showed GMC cars: "The car models shown are GMC, not Buick"; Gladiator, Cherokee). Body-style names follow the dealer site ("Passenger Vans", "Sedans" not "Cars"). The slider includes every body style the dealer sells (Sentra, Ram 2500: "add New RAM Hybrids").
- **CTAs.** All four CTA buttons must be present, including the bottom CTA row (bZ: "you missed the bottom CTAs"; Certified Pre-Owned).
- **Spacing and alignment.** Keep spacing consistent between sections, with no oversized gaps (Canyon, Encore GX, Envista, Sentra, Blazer). Single centred blocks are centre-aligned (Ram 1500, Trax, Blazer).
- **Images.** Vehicle photos must not be cut off or too tight: "zoom out these photos" (Ram 1500 mobile, Diesel Truck Service). Photos must match the OEM model and trim.
- **Punctuation.** Copy ends with a full stop; check the hero title for missing commas (Ram 1500, Santa Fe).
- **Missing spaces in headings** (Huston CDJR, 2026-11-13: "Please fix the H2: 'Why Davenport Drivers ChooseHuston CDJR' ... Add a space"). Every h1-h4 needs a space between every word; check line joins (`<br>`, `<span>`) too. Fixed directly, not asked (user rule). `qa_sweep.js` checks it.

Fonts render wider in the browser than in Figma's text box, so a rule as wide as the Figma box (433) ended about 70px short of the title (504 at 1920). Size the title column to its content (`width:max-content` with the Figma width as `min-width`) so the rule always matches the title, and check rule width against title text width at 1920 and 1366.
- **MLP content title + top margin** (Pacifica, Ram 2500, Cherokee, Gladiator, Durango, 2026-11-12/13): if the content doc has a title line, show it as a bold h2 above the first question at the same size as the rest (user rule, settled 2026-11-12). The space above the MLP content must equal the page's left margin at every width (80 design-px at 1920, 4vw on tablet, 20px on phones).

The Figma used 'Style & sophistication' on two cards; the second was really the LED lighting feature. ## Mobile (Final Mobile Check)

- The Explore and Discover sections use the same mobile layout as the other MLPs (Ram 1500, 2026-08-24). Use the standard Trax-style mobile layout.
- Section sliders stay sliders on mobile ("add a slider to this section, similar to the other MLPs").
- Zoom out photos so the whole vehicle is visible.
- **Zoom in short, wide feature photos** (2027 Chrysler Pacifica, Huston CDJR, 2026-11-12: "Please zoom-in this image", the Stow 'n Vac photo). A 2.4:1 photo shown whole at 390px is too small and showed baked-in white edges. Give it a mobile crop (`-m.webp`, about 3:2) around the action (hands, tool, seat), with the white edges removed and nothing of the subject cut. Check every wide feature photo this way, not only the one QA flagged.
- **Gradient under mobile photos** (2027 Chrysler Pacifica, Huston CDJR, 2026-11-12: "Please fix the gradient effect here" on three photo panels). A flat `--edge` colour fading to black over 48px read as a grey or tan band, and the white heading sat on it. Fix: the panel continues the photo's own bottom colours across the width (12 sampled stops, `--edgeg`), fades to black within about 48px, and the heading starts below the fade (36px top padding). The photo itself stays untouched. The toolkit components still use the flat `--edge` band, so update them the same way.
- **Never crop a car photo on phones** (2027 Chevrolet Corvette, Huston Chevrolet, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-13: "Please zoom out this image", 9 screenshots). A blanket CSS 2:1 cover crop on 15 wide photos (about 2.9:1 and 3.7:1) cut the noses, tails and second cars off. Fix: vehicle photos show whole on phones (`aspect-ratio:auto`, img `height:auto`) and carry `data-whole="1"` so `qa_sweep.js` skips its "zoom in" check; only detail photos with no car to cut (exhaust, wheel, interior) keep a crop. Before using any phone crop, look at each cropped photo at 390: if any part of a vehicle leaves the frame, show it whole instead.
- **Mobile heading-to-content gaps about 12-16px** (2026 Chevrolet Blazer, Huston Chevrolet, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-13: "Reduce spacing here" on 8 spots - heading -> photo, subtitle -> cards/icons/photos, 'Connection on command' -> its text, 'Dialed in' photo -> title -> tiles). The generic 20/30px section gaps read as too loose under a heading. Heading/subtitle -> next block 12px, h2 -> its own subtitle 10px, card padding not added on top (icon cards 0 top). `qa_sweep.js` prints CHECK for any phone heading gap over 22px.
- **Small tile sets stay compact on phones** (Blazer mobile 2026-11-13: "Make this two rows", 6 drive-mode tiles shown 2/1/2/1). 6 short tiles (icon + 1-2 words) go 3 across = two rows (gap 10px, tiles stretched to equal height, title 15px), not the 2-column odd-centred pattern. Use 2 columns only when the tile text needs it.
- **Mobile hero: photo to title spacing** (2027 Ram 2500, Huston CDJR, 2026-11-12: "Please slightly reduce the spacing here", the gap between the hero photo and the title in the black panel). 24px panel padding plus the title's 10px rule padding gave about 33px from the photo to the title glyphs. Keep it about 20px (Ram: 12px panel top padding, about 21px visible) to match the mobile 20px rhythm.
- **Centre the vehicle photo in its band on phones** (Encore GX, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-16: "Center the image"). The car photo sat flush against the top of the dark band (0px above, 20px below). A vehicle photo inside a coloured band gets the same space above as below (20px) on phones.
- **Break long trims titles at "Build & Price" on phones** (Encore GX, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-16: "Put the Build & down"). '2026 Buick Encore GX Build &' / 'Price' broke after the ampersand. Write the title as two block spans with a space between them (`<span>2026 Buick Encore GX</span> <span>Build &amp; Price</span>`, display:block at <=1024) so phones show the model on one line and "Build & Price" on the next; desktop stays one line.
- **Mobile hero: whole vehicle, centred** (2026 Chevrolet Trax, Huston Chevrolet, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-16: "Please zoom out and center the Hero Image"). The 3:2 phone frame showed the middle of the photo and cut off the back of the car, which sat right of centre in the file. Centre the phone crop on the vehicle (not on the file) and zoom out until the whole vehicle shows with some margin (vehicle about 75-80% of the width; Trax: 7:4 frame, `translateX(-57.5%)`). Check the hero at 390 on every page: no part of the vehicle may leave the frame.
- **Captions under phone photos sit centred** (Trax, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-16: "Shift this downward so it will be centered" on six safety captions, 10px under the photo and 20px to the next card). Same space above and below a caption (15px / 15px).

Band bottom 20px, heading -> content 16px (see "Mobile heading-to-content gaps" above; `qa_sweep.js` now also measures headings followed by a `display:contents` wrapper, which it skipped before).
- **Wide service-bay photos with a car: show whole on phones** (Audi Stuart Service Hub, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-19: "please zoom-out this image" - the "What We Offer" photo, 2.6:1, cropped to 3:2 cut the car's nose and the technician). Same rule as "Never crop a car photo on phones": `aspect-ratio:auto; height:auto` + `data-whole="1"`, check every wide section photo, not only the flagged one.
- **Smaller card buttons and tighter section gaps on phones** (Helms Brothers Research Page, Final Mobile Check 2026-11-19: "Please reduce the spacing here" above "Our Best Sellers" - 30px; "please reduce the size of these CTA buttons. Check and update the other cards as well" - VIEW INVENTORY 190x48 at 14px).
